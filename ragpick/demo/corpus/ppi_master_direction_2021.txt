Master Direction on Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPIs)

All Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuers

Madam / Dear Sir,

1. Introduction
Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPIs) facilitate purchase of goods and services, financial services and remittances against the value stored on such instruments.

2. Limits on PPIs
The limits applicable to each category of PPI are given in the table below.

| PPI category | Maximum amount outstanding | Monthly loading limit |
| Small PPI (minimum details) | Rs 10,000 | Rs 10,000 |
| Full-KYC PPI | Rs 2,00,000 | Not specified |
| Gift PPI | Rs 10,000 | Not reloadable |

3. Validity
All PPIs issued in the country shall have a minimum validity period of one year from the date of last loading or reloading in the PPI.

4. Cash Withdrawal
Cash withdrawal shall be permitted only from full-KYC PPIs, subject to a limit of Rs 2,000 per transaction and an overall limit of Rs 10,000 per month per PPI.
