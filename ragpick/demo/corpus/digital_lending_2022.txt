Guidelines on Digital Lending

All Commercial Banks and NBFCs

Madam / Dear Sir,

1. Disbursal and Repayment
All loan disbursals and repayments shall be executed only between the bank accounts of the borrower and the regulated entity, without any pass-through or pool account of any third party.

2. Key Fact Statement
A standardised Key Fact Statement (KFS) shall be provided to the borrower before the execution of the contract. The KFS shall disclose the all-inclusive cost of digital loans in the form of the Annual Percentage Rate (APR).

3. Cooling-off Period
Borrowers shall be given an explicit option to exit the digital loan by paying the principal and the proportionate APR without any penalty during the cooling-off period. The cooling-off period shall not be less than three days for loans having tenor of seven days or more, and one day for loans having tenor of less than seven days.

4. Grievance Redressal
Regulated entities shall appoint a nodal grievance redressal officer to deal with digital lending complaints. If a complaint is not resolved within a period of thirty days, the borrower can lodge a complaint under the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
