Credit Card Operations of Banks

All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Madam / Dear Sir,

1. Closure of Credit Card
Any request for closure of a credit card shall be honoured within ten working days by the card-issuer, subject to payment of all dues by the cardholder.

2. Billing
Card-issuers shall ensure that there is no delay in dispatching bills and the customer has sufficient number of days, at least fourteen days, for making payment before the interest starts getting charged.

3. Unsolicited Cards
Unsolicited cards shall not be issued. In case an unsolicited card is issued and activated without the consent of the recipient, the card-issuer shall reverse the charges immediately.
