Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card - Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022

All Scheduled Commercial Banks and NBFCs

Madam / Dear Sir,

1. These Directions are issued in supersession of circular DBR.FSD.BC.No.5/24.01.011/2019-20 dated July 1, 2019 on Credit Card Operations of Banks.

2. Closure of Credit Card
2.1 Any request for closure of a credit card shall be honoured within seven working days by the card-issuer, subject to payment of all dues by the cardholder.
2.2 Failure on the part of the card-issuer to complete the process of closure within seven working days shall result in a penalty of Rs 500 per day of delay payable to the customer, till the closure of the account.

3. Billing
3.1 Card-issuers shall ensure that the customer has at least fifteen days for making payment before the interest starts getting charged.
3.2 Card-issuers shall not levy any charge that was not explicitly indicated to the cardholder at the time of issue of the card.

4. Unsolicited Cards
4.1 Issue of unsolicited cards is strictly prohibited. If an unsolicited card is issued and activated without the explicit consent of the recipient, the card-issuer shall reverse the charges immediately and pay a penalty of twice the value of the charges reversed.
