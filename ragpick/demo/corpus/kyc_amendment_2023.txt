Amendment to Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016

All Regulated Entities

Madam / Dear Sir,

1. Please refer to paragraph 38 of the Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 dated February 25, 2016 on periodic updation of KYC.

2. On a review, it has been decided to amend the periodicity for low risk customers. Periodic updation for low risk customers shall now be carried out at least once every eight years. The periodicity for high risk and medium risk customers remains unchanged.

3. In case of no change in KYC information, a self-declaration from a low risk individual customer may be obtained through registered email, registered mobile number, ATMs, digital channels or a letter, and no fresh documents shall be required.

4. These amendments shall come into force with immediate effect.

Yours faithfully,
Chief General Manager
